After applying the four‐step protocol to the Cachoeira interfluve imagery, I find no evidence of buried, pre‐Columbian water‐management features. The prominent linear and rectilinear patterns visible in the DTM, Sentinel-2 visual, and NIR composites all correspond to modern clearings, plantation edges, and recent access tracks. These align precisely with the OpenStreetMap vectors and thus can be masked out as recent infrastructure.

No anomalies meeting the criteria for raised fields, canals, ditches, embankments or causeways under continuous forest canopy were detected in this sector.
