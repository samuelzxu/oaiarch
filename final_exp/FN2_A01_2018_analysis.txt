1. Region and Historical Context  
   The Feliz Natal interfluvial region of Mato Grosso lies between the Arinos and Juruena river systems. Pre-Columbian societies here engineered extensive lowland platforms and canals to cope with seasonal flooding and nutrient-poor soils. Terra preta (anthropogenic dark earth) sites and raised-field networks are well documented in more easterly Amazonian interfluves, but this western zone has been under-studied.
