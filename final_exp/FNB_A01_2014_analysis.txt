After applying a multi‐step workflow (multi‐azimuth hillshade/slope/curvature analysis, NIR/visible spectral cross-checking, and modern‐feature masking via OSM) over the Travessão Central interfluve, all of the linear and rectilinear signatures visible in the LiDAR‐derived hillshade coincide precisely with modern deforestation and plantation parcels (as confirmed by Sentinel-2 visual/NIR and the OpenStreetMap layer). No upland water‐management structures of pre-Columbian origin (raised fields, canals, embankments, causeways or ditches) were detected beneath the canopy.
